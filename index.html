Case Note · App Store Compliance

Architected App Store listing requirements & privacy policy strategy

For PassGen, a macOS menu bar password generator: wrote the App Store Connect metadata end to end, then designed and published a privacy policy built around a genuinely simple fact — the app collects nothing.

Project: PassGen Platform: macOS Status: Live
0 DATA POINTS COLLECTED

$0 POLICY HOSTING COST

App Store submission needs more than a working build. Every listing field — name, subtitle, description, keywords, category — has a character limit and a job to do, and every app needs a privacy policy Apple can link to, whether or not it touches user data.

PassGen doesn't touch user data. No accounts, no network calls, no analytics. That made the privacy policy less about disclosure and more about stating a fact clearly: passwords are generated on-device, copied to the system clipboard like any other copy action, and cleared automatically after 90 seconds — but only if the clipboard still holds that exact password, so it never wipes out something else the person copied since.

Listing Requirements

✓Name & subtitleWithin Apple's 30-character subtitle limit
✓Description copyFeature-led, written for the App Store page
✓KeywordsFit to the 100-character field limit
✓CategoryUtilities
✓Age rating & copyrightSet for submission

Privacy Strategy

✓Zero data collectionStated plainly, not buried in legal language
✓Clipboard handling disclosedExplains the 90-second auto-clear safeguard
✓No third-party trackingNo analytics or ad SDKs to disclose
✓Published & linkableLive at a public URL for App Store Connect
