PassGen Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

PassGen is a macOS menu bar utility for generating secure passwords. This policy explains, plainly, what data PassGen does and does not handle.

Data Collection

PassGen does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics of any kind. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no network connections made by the app at all.

Generated Passwords

Passwords are generated entirely on your device, in memory, and are never saved to disk, logged, or sent anywhere. When you copy a password, it is placed on your Mac's system clipboard, the same as any other copy action, and PassGen automatically clears the clipboard 90 seconds later, but only if it still contains the exact password that was copied.

Third-Party Services

PassGen does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

PassGen does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, since it collects no information at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
